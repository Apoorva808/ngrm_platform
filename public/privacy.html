Your data

Privacy notice

What NGRM collects, why, who can see it, and how to take it back. Written to be read in two minutes.

Who runs this

What we collect, and only with your consent

  • Account: your name, email and college, given by you when you sign up or added by your college or the pilot team. If you choose a password, only a scrambled form of it is kept.
  • Profile: programme, year, CGPA, target role, your skill ratings and career-clarity rating.
  • Evidence: courses, certificates, projects, internships and roles you add, and any proof files you upload.
  • Personality results: only if you separately agree, and only once you enter all five traits yourself.
  • Your choices: which plan steps you accepted or declined, and each consent you gave or withdrew.

Each use has its own consent, and you can say no to any of them except the profile, which the service cannot work without. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree.

What we do with it

  • Work out your readiness score with a fixed, published formula. No AI decides your score.
  • Suggest next steps. You accept or decline each one; a mentor cannot override a decline.
  • If you agree, an AI service rewords the reason for a step. It never receives your name or email.
  • If you agree, include your figures, without your name, in the outcome study.

Who can see what

  • You: everything, including a full download.
  • Your mentor: your profile, evidence, proof files and plan. Your mentor confirms your CGPA against college records and records your assessment results.
  • Your college’s placement cell: rounded totals only. It sees your name only if you say you are interested in an employer’s introduction request.
  • Employers: rounded totals only. Never your name, profile or score. Groups under 5 are hidden.
  • Researchers: figures under a code, not your name, and only if you joined the study.
  • Admins: account details and a log of actions, to run the service.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your rights

  • See and download everything we hold about you, on the Evidence and privacy page.
  • Correct your profile and evidence at any time.
  • Withdraw any consent. That use stops; withdrawing personality consent also removes those results from earlier scores.
  • Delete your account. Your profile, evidence, files, scores and plan are removed for good.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account or the pilot ends, whichever comes first. Sign-in sessions last 7 days. Your password is kept only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back.
