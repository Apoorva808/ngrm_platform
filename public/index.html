Know where you stand. Know what to do next.

NGRM gives every student a readiness score that shows its working, a semester-by-semester plan they can accept or turn down, and a faculty mentor who reviews it. Colleges see where the curriculum falls short. Employers see skill supply in totals, never a student’s name, profile or score.
